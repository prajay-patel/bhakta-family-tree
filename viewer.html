❧ Our Family History

The Bhakta family belongs to the Bhakta (Ramkabir) branch of the Leuva Patidar — a community whose journey spans Central Asia, the Indian subcontinent, East Africa, and the modern diaspora.

East Africa

From the early 1900s, Patidar families settled across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, and South Africa — developing farming and retail sectors under British colonial rule. Many Bhakta family members made this journey, creating the trans-generational family this tree captures.

South Gujarat — 243 villages

The Leuva Patidar heartland spans the Surat, Navsari, Valsad, and Tapi districts of South Gujarat. The tree covers all 243 known Leuva Patidar villages in this region — if a node shows a village of origin, it links directly to this heritage.
