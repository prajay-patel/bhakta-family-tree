# Privacy Notice

*Last updated: 2025*

This notice explains what information is collected when you use this form,
how it is stored, who can see it, and what your rights are.

---

## Who is the data controller

This family tree project is run by **[Your Name]**, reachable at
**[your-email@example.com]**. There is no company or organisation involved —
this is a personal project for family use.

---

## What information is collected

When you submit the form, the following may be collected:

- Your name and email address (as the person submitting)
- Names, dates of birth, places of birth, and dates of death for the family
  members you describe
- Village or ancestral origin
- Country of emigration
- Gotra (patrilineal clan)
- Relationship terms (e.g. Masi, Dada) describing how people are connected
- Any notes or stories you choose to add

Nothing is required except your own first and last name as the submitter.
Every other field is optional — fill in only what you are comfortable sharing.

---

## How information is stored

Your submission travels directly from your browser to a private Google Sheet
using an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It does not pass through or touch this
GitHub repository at any point.

The Google Sheet is:
- Stored in the curator's personal Google Drive
- Not shared with anyone else
- Not publicly accessible
- Subject to Google's own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

A local copy of the data is maintained on the curator's personal computer for
processing. This local copy is not uploaded to any cloud service other than the
Google Sheet described above.

---

## Who can see your information

Only the tree curator (**[Your Name]**) can access the raw submissions.

The processed family tree — which shows names, approximate dates, and
relationships — may be shared with other family members via the viewer page
(`viewer.html`). If you would prefer certain information not appear in the
shared viewer, please note this when submitting or contact the curator directly.

---

## How long information is kept

Submitted information is kept for as long as the family tree project is active.
There is no automatic deletion schedule. You may request deletion at any time
(see below).

---

## Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you have the right to:

**See your data** — ask the curator to show you everything stored about you
or submitted by you.

**Correct your data** — if any stored information is wrong, the curator will
correct it.

**Delete your data** — if you want your submission removed, the curator will
delete it from all stored copies (raw archive, processed records, and the
viewer tree) and confirm when done.

**Object to processing** — if you have concerns about how your information
is being used, contact the curator to discuss.

To exercise any of these rights, email **[your-email@example.com]**. Requests
will be responded to within 30 days.

---

## Legal basis for processing (EU/UK GDPR)

This project falls under the personal or household activity exemption in
Article 2(2)(c) of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR. It is operated by a private
individual solely for personal and family purposes, with no commercial
dimension and no disclosure to an indefinite number of persons.

---

## Cookies and tracking

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of
any kind. The only external connection made when using this form is to
Google's servers (to receive your submission) and to Google Fonts (to load
the Playfair Display and Inter typefaces).

## Changes to this notice

If this notice is updated, the date at the top will change. For significant
changes, the curator will notify family members who have provided an email
address.
